About Apex Air, Heating & Electric

Woman-owned. Licensed. Insured. 12+ years serving the Central Valley — and 246+ five-star reviews that say we do it right.

Apex General Contracting, Inc. — operating as Apex Air, Heating & Electric — is a woman-owned HVAC and electrical contractor based in Fresno, CA. We've been keeping Central Valley homes cool in the summer, warm in the winter, and powered up year-round since 2014.

Our specialties

  • Electrical panel upgrades, whole-home rewiring, and electrical safety inspections
  • AC repair and installation, heating and furnace service, and maintenance plans
  • EV charger installation, lighting, outlets, and switches
  • 24/7 emergency electrical and HVAC repair across the Central Valley

Trusted, verified, rated

  • 5.0-star Google rating across 246+ reviews
  • Best of Central California honoree 2023–2026
  • BBB A+ rated — BuildZoom score 97 (top 18% of CA contractors)
  • Woman-owned, led by licensed contractor Beatrice Pino

Who we serve

Fresno, Clovis, Madera, Madera Ranchos, Sanger, Kingsburg, Fowler, Kerman, Visalia, and the wider Central Valley — residential and light commercial, from a 1,400 sq ft Clovis ranch to a 3,200 sq ft Fig Garden two-story.
