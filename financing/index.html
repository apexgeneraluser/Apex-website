Financing Questions, Answered

Will checking my options affect my credit score?
No. Pre-qualification uses a soft credit check, which does not affect your credit score. You see estimated monthly payment options before deciding whether to move forward.
How much can I finance?
The calculator handles project costs from $1,000 to $100,000 — from a single repair up to a full HVAC replacement or a whole-home electrical panel upgrade.
How fast can I be approved and funded?
Pre-qualification takes about two minutes, and approved homeowners can receive funds in as little as 24 hours. Once funding clears, we schedule the work.
What projects can be financed?
Any Apex project over $1,000: AC installation and replacement, furnace and heat pump installation, electrical panel upgrades, whole-home rewiring, EV charger installation, and emergency repairs.
Do I need perfect credit to qualify?
No. Hearth's lending partners work with a wide range of credit profiles, including FICO scores as low as 550. Qualifying homeowners may also see 0% introductory APR credit card options with terms from 6 to 18 months.
