Common Questions

Answers before you call

Straight answers to the questions Central Valley homeowners ask us most.

What areas does Apex serve?

We serve Fresno, Clovis, Madera, Visalia and the wider Central Valley — including Sanger, Kingsburg, Fowler and Kerman. Call (559) 210-9405 to confirm availability for your address.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency electrical and HVAC repair?

Yes. Our phone line is answered 24/7 and a licensed technician is on call for power outages, sparking panels, AC failures in extreme heat, and furnace breakdowns. Call (559) 210-9405 anytime.

Are you licensed and insured for electrical and HVAC work in California?

Yes. Apex holds CSLB license #1072290 with full insurance, and carries a 5.0-star Google rating across 246+ reviews.

Do you install EV chargers?

Yes. Level 2 EV charger installs are sized to your panel and vehicle, including any panel upgrade your home needs first. See our electrical services for details.

How fast can I get service in Fresno?

Most customers are seen same-day or next-day. Book online in under a minute or call (559) 210-9405 to check today's availability.

What financial options do you offer?

We offer straightforward pricing and financing options on qualifying HVAC and electrical projects. Call (559) 210-9405 for a free estimate.
