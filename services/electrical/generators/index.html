Frequently Asked Questions About Generator Installation

How much does a standby generator cost in Fresno, CA?
Most Fresno homeowners invest between $8,500 and $16,000 for a whole-home standby generator installation in 2026. A 16-22kW system — enough for most Central Valley homes — typically runs $8,500 to $13,000 installed, while a 24-38kW system for larger homes with air conditioning, EV charging, and well pumps on the essential load runs $13,000 to $18,000. Portable and manual-start backup systems run $2,500 to $5,000. Every Apex quote includes permit fees, PG&E coordination, the concrete pad, and a full 1-year workmanship warranty.
What size generator do I need for my house?
Generator size is set by a load calculation, not by square footage. We add up what you actually need to keep running — air conditioning, well pump, refrigeration, medical equipment, or a home office — and size the system to that load with the starting surge of large motors like compressors and pumps accounted for. Most Fresno homes land on a 16-22kW whole-home standby system; larger homes with AC, EV charging, and a well pump on the essential load need 24-38kW. We perform the load calculation as part of a free sizing assessment.
What is the difference between a standby and a portable generator?
A standby generator is permanently installed outside your home, runs on natural gas or propane, and starts automatically within seconds of an outage — it powers your whole home without you being there. A portable or manual-start system is a lower-cost option sized for essential circuits only; you start it and connect it through a manual transfer switch, which makes it a practical choice for renters and smaller homes.
Do you handle the permits and PG&E coordination for a generator install?
Yes. Apex Air, Heating & Electric handles the entire process from start to finish: the electrical load calculation, the City of Fresno or Fresno County permit application, PG&E coordination, the gas or propane fuel line run, installation, and final inspection sign-off. All work is performed by licensed electricians and permitted for inspection.
Do you service and maintain generators after installation?
Yes. Annual service plans include scheduled load-bank testing, oil changes, and battery checks, so your generator starts when you actually need it. A standby generator that has sat untested for two years is the most common reason a backup system fails during an outage. Apex Air, Heating & Electric maintains residential, commercial, and agricultural standby systems throughout the Central Valley.
