Residential Electrician Services in Fresno & Clovis

Fresno has tens of thousands of homes built between 1950 and 1990 in neighborhoods like the Tower District, Old Fig Garden, Woodward Park, and Sunnyside. Many of these properties still operate on 60-amp or 100-amp electrical panels that were never engineered to support today's electrical lifestyle — central air conditioning, electric heat pumps, EV chargers, computers, and induction cooking.

Warning Signs You Need a Licensed Electrician:

  • Frequent Breaker Trips: Your circuit breakers click off whenever the microwave, hair dryer, or AC kicks on simultaneously.
  • Flickering or Dimming Lights: Lights fluctuate in brightness when heavy appliances cycle on, pointing to overloaded circuits or loose neutral conductors.
  • Discolored or Warm Outlets: Receptacles that feel hot to the touch, exhibit scorch marks, or spark when plugging in cords.
  • Outdated Fuse Boxes or Obsolete Panels: Panels manufactured by Federal Pacific Electric (FPE Stab-Lok) or Zinsco are known fire hazards that insurance carriers often require to be replaced.
  • Lack of Grounding (Two-Prong Outlets): Older homes without dedicated equipment grounding conductors leave delicate electronics vulnerable to voltage spikes.
