Immediate Safety First

What to Do Right Now (Before We Arrive)

  • Electrical Sparks or Burning Odor: If it is safe to do so, shut off the main breaker or the individual branch circuit breaker. Never touch standing water near a wet electrical panel or outlet. Call us immediately at (559) 210-9405.
  • Natural Gas Smell (Rotten Eggs): Immediately evacuate all family members and pets from the structure. Do not turn light switches on or off, and do not use a phone inside. Call 911 from outside, then call our emergency team.
  • AC Failure in Extreme Triple-Digit Heat: Close blinds and curtains, move vulnerable individuals to the coolest interior room, and call us for prioritized emergency cooling dispatch.

Electrical Emergencies We Handle 24/7

Immediate Danger

Sparks, Smoke & Burning Smells

Any smoke or burning odor from an outlet, light switch, or breaker box is an active fire warning. According to the U.S. Fire Administration, electrical malfunctions trigger ~24,000 house fires annually. We isolate and repair the fault safely.

Service Outage

Complete or Partial Power Outages

If your home lost power while neighboring houses still have lights, the failure is inside your service panel, meter base, or main breaker. We trace and restore power fast.

Arcing Hazard

Buzzing or Crackling Breaker Panels

A breaker panel that hums, crackles, or feels hot indicates a failing breaker, loose utility neutral, or arcing bus bar. Left untreated, it will melt connections and start a fire.

Shock Risk

Exposed Damaged Wiring & Outlets

Chewed wires, damaged exterior conduit, scorched outlet covers, or fixtures that deliver shocks when touched require immediate professional de-energizing and repair.

HVAC Emergencies We Handle 24/7

Summer Priority

Complete AC Failure in Extreme Heat

Valley temperatures routinely hit 105°F–110°F+. A broken AC creates real health risks for seniors, infants, and pets. We prioritize emergency cooling calls across Fresno.

Winter Safety

Furnace Breakdown on Freezing Nights

Sudden heating failure during freezing Central Valley nights leaves homes dangerously cold. Our technicians troubleshoot burners, gas valves, ignitors, and blower motors fast.

Health Alert

Burning Odor or Carbon Monoxide Alert

If your CO detector alarms or your vents blow an electrical burning smell, shut off the thermostat immediately. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks and test exhaust venting.

Property Protection

AC Condensate Overflow Flooding

Blocked drain lines cause drain pans in attics to overflow, threatening to collapse drywall ceilings. We clear emergency blockages and install safety float switches.
