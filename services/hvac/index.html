Common AC Breakdowns in Fresno — What We Fix Every Day

Continuous triple-digit heat places extreme thermal and electrical stress on outdoor condensers and blower motors. The most frequent causes of cooling failure include:

Blown Run Capacitors

Capacitors provide the electrical kick to start compressor and fan motors. Overheating causes them to swell and fail. We carry universal replacements on every truck.

Refrigerant Leaks

Low refrigerant means your coil freezes into a block of ice. Adding Freon without locating the leak is a waste; we pinpoint and repair leaks using electronic detectors.

Clogged Condensate Lines

Valley dust and algae block drain lines, triggering float switches that shut down your entire system to prevent water damage to ceilings. We flush and clear drains fast.

Dirty Coils & Restricted Airflow

Dust from agricultural surroundings coats outdoor coils, preventing heat dissipation and causing high-pressure head trips. Chemical coil cleaning restores factory capacity.

In the Fresno market, a complete residential HVAC system replacement typically ranges from $9,000 for a straightforward AC-only unit to $14,000–$22,000 for a complete high-efficiency dual-fuel or heat pump split system with new ductwork. Apex provides itemized written proposals with no pressure and flexible financing options.
How do I know whether to repair or replace my AC unit?
We recommend following the 'Rule of 5,000': multiply the age of your equipment in years by the estimated repair cost. If the result exceeds $5,000, replacement is generally the more economical path. Additionally, if your system is over 12–15 years old, uses phased-out R-22 Freon, or has suffered a major compressor breakdown, a modern SEER2 unit will dramatically reduce monthly electric bills while restoring comfort.
What does an HVAC maintenance plan include?
Apex preventative maintenance plans include two comprehensive 21-point seasonal tune-ups per year: a Spring cooling inspection (measuring refrigerant pressures, testing capacitors, cleaning coils, clearing condensate drains) and a Fall heating tune-up (testing heat exchangers for carbon monoxide cracks, inspecting burners, calibrating thermostats, lubricating blowers). Members also receive priority emergency scheduling and discounts on repairs.
